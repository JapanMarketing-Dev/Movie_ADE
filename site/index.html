How it works

From one take to a finished video, without a timeline.

Every step is a command your agent runs. The template decides the order, and solocut next always tells the agent what to do now.

  1. Pick a template

    TikTok talk, YouTube tutorial, Shorts from a long video… Format, layout, cut policy and caption style are set.

  2. Record

    Screen, camera and mic at once, each on its own track. Add footage from another camera later.

  3. Ask your agent

    “Edit this.” Claude Code or Codex follows the template: sync, transcribe, rough cut, captions, layout.

  4. Decision model decides

    Cut or keep, which retake, where captions break. Only uncertain items come to you.

  5. Check every frame

    Cut boundaries, black frames, caption safe areas, faces, personal info on screen, mosaic tracking.

  6. Talk over the preview

    Circle what bothers you and say it. It reaches your agent as timecoded instructions. Then export.

Record

Screen and camera at once. Another camera, synced later.

Each source is saved on its own track, so the template can switch between picture-in-picture, split screen, camera only or screen only at any point.

  • Screen, cameras, mics and system audio recorded together on one clock
  • Import footage from a mirrorless camera or iPhone; it syncs to the frame by audio, with drift correction for long takes
  • Teleprompter, countdown, and hotkeys to mark “bad take” or “important” while you talk
  • Crash-safe: files are written every second

Templates

Decide once how your videos are made.

A template fixes the look, the editing rules written as prompts, and the pipeline. Any agent follows the same steps and the same quality bar, because Solocut generates the agent’s instructions from it.

  • Built-in: tech tutorial, tech short, talk for YouTube, talk for TikTok, Shorts from a long video
  • Tech templates zoom to where you click, show the keys you press and highlight the cursor
  • Shooting with several cameras? Write how to switch angles in plain words; your agent follows the prompt
  • solocut next enforces the steps and their pass conditions
# templates/tiktok-talk/template.yaml
id: tiktok-talk
canvas: { width: 1080, height: 1920, fps: 30 }
layouts: [camera-full, screen-top-camera-bottom]
cut:
  silence: { minMs: 300, padMs: 80 }
  fillers: [um, uh, like]
  retakes: keep-last
captions: { style: tiktok-bold, highlight: word, safeArea: tiktok }
audio: { loudness: -14, truePeak: -1 }
checks: [cut-boundaries, caption-safe-area, pii-on-screen]
pipeline:
  [ingest, sync, transcribe, rough-cut, captions,
   layout, preview, check, review, final]

Terminal

Your agent, your subscription, your terminal.

Solocut runs agent CLIs in real terminals inside the app. Switch between them in tabs; the project state lives in files, so any agent can pick up where another stopped.

  • Any CLI agent

    Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, OpenCode, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, or your own command.

  • Switch and hand off

    Hit a usage limit? The next agent opens with the handoff note and continues.

  • Always up to date

    Solocut updates itself, signed, from Cloudflare. It also tells you when your agent CLIs have a new version.

  • Headless CLI

    solocut works without the app, too: in your own terminal or in CI.

  • Every edit is undoable

    Edits are operations in a log. See who changed what and why, and roll back.

  • Originals stay untouched

    Recorded footage is read-only. Everything else can be rebuilt.

Decision model

A final check on every frame that matters.

Machine checks run first (black frames, freezes, loudness, specs). Then a decision model looks at the frames around every cut, every caption change and every mosaic, and answers typed questions with a probability. Confident answers are applied; uncertain ones come to you.

cut 00:41:07mid-word? 0.58 → you
frame 01:02:11email visible → mosaic
  • Runs locally with Ollama, or with your own API key (TypeSafe, OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Workers AI)
  • Spending limits per project and per day, with a log of every call
  • Only frames and transcript text are sent, and only after you agree

Mosaic

Personal info, hidden and tracked.

Emails, notifications, API keys, phone numbers, bystanders’ faces and license plates are detected and tracked, then pixelated, blurred or filled. Masks follow the source, so they stay in place when you change the layout or reframe to 9:16.

Feedback by video

Don’t type the fix. Point at it.

Play the preview, talk, pause and circle. Solocut transcribes on your machine and turns each remark into a timecode, a frame with your pen, your words and the exact caption or clip you meant. One click sends it to your agent.

Benchmark

Paste a URL. Borrow the pattern, not the video.

Open a reference video — your own, or one you have permission to study — in the built-in browser. While you play it, Solocut measures how captions appear, where sound effects land, how loud the music sits under the voice and how fast the cuts come, then turns it into a template you can use. Nothing is downloaded or saved; only numbers are kept.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. Solocut is MIT-licensed. The editing intelligence comes from the agent subscription you already have (Claude Code, Codex…), and the decision model can run locally.

Can I use it today?

Not yet. Solocut is being designed and built in the open. Watch the GitHub repository to get the first release.

Do I need to know how to edit?

No. You record and talk. The template and your agent do the editing; you review the result.

Is Remotion required?

No. The render engine is FFmpeg. Remotion is an optional add-on for motion graphics, and Solocut shows its license terms before installing it. HyperFrames and Revideo are alternatives.

Which platforms can I export for?

YouTube (16:9, up to 4K), TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels (9:16), with captions, chapters and metadata.

Can it post for me?

Yes. Solocut uploads to YouTube and TikTok and announces the video on X, Instagram, Threads and Facebook, always after you check and approve the post.
